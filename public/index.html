How this site works

Every hour, a script pulls fresh listings from Adzuna, Reed, and a handful of company career boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby), filters them down to genuinely entry-level, UK-based roles, tags each one with a category and (for tech roles) a detected tech stack, and stores them in a hosted database.

The listing you're looking at right now is served by a small Node.js/Express backend, with a plain HTML/CSS/JS frontend and no build step. Nothing here scrapes LinkedIn or Indeed directly - just their public, ToS-friendly APIs, plus employers' own official career board APIs.
